Key Crypto-Specific Metrics Included

    Market Status Card: Shows overall crypto market sentiment (Bullish/Bearish/Neutral)

    Fear & Greed Index: Replaces volatility with crypto-specific sentiment indicator

    Total Market Cap: Tracks the entire cryptocurrency market capitalization

    Bitcoin Dominance: Shows BTC's percentage of total crypto market cap

    24h Trading Volume: Total trading volume across all cryptocurrencies

    Market Cap Distribution: Breakdown of Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Altcoin market share

    Top Movers: Lists best and worst performing cryptocurrencies

    Asset Details: Technical analysis for individual cryptocurrencies when selected

Additional Crypto Metrics to Calculate

To make this dashboard even more insightful, consider calculating these crypto-specific metrics:

    Altcoin Season Index: Measures whether altcoins are outperforming Bitcoin

    Stablecoin Dominance: Tracks the percentage of trading volume in stablecoins

    Exchange Flows: Measures inflows/outflows from exchanges (indicator of buying/selling pressure)

    MVRV Ratio: Market Value to Realized Value ratio (indicates if assets are over/undervalued)

    Network Activity: On-chain metrics like active addresses, transaction volume

    Social Sentiment: Analysis of social media mentions and sentiment

    DeFi Metrics: Total Value Locked (TVL) across different protocols and chains

    Funding Rates: Perpetual swap market funding rates across exchanges

This cryptocurrency dashboard provides traders with a comprehensive overview of the crypto market while maintaining the original styling and structure of your template. Users can click on any cryptocurrency to view detailed analysis, making it both informative and interactive.
