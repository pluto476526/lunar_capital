Additional Metrics to Calculate

To make this dashboard more insightful, consider calculating these additional forex-specific metrics:

    Currency Strength Index - Calculate the relative strength of each major currency

    Session Overlap Volatility - Measure volatility during overlapping trading sessions

    Carry Trade Opportunities - Identify pairs with significant interest rate differentials

    Correlation Matrix - Calculate correlations between major currency pairs

    Average True Range (ATR) - Measure volatility for each currency pair

    Pivot Points - Calculate support and resistance levels for major pairs

    Order Flow Analysis - Track buy/sell pressure in the market

This enhanced dashboard provides a comprehensive market overview while allowing users to drill down into specific pairs for more detailed analysis, all without changing the original styling or adding buy/sell signals.
